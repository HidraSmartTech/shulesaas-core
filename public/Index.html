Platform Features

Everything Your School Needs

From score entry to printed report cards — every academic workflow built into one clean, modern system.

01

Score Submission

Teachers enter and update exam scores with real-time validation, subject-level accuracy, and admin-controlled deadlines.

02

Attendance Tracking

Morning and afternoon attendance with weekly/monthly summaries, automatic reports, and teacher-on-duty scheduling.

03

Class Reports

Full examination result reports with division analysis, grade distribution, and subject performance breakdowns.

04

Student Report Cards

Individual A4 report cards with grades, behaviour scores, teacher signatures, and term-by-term progress tracking.

05

Parent Portal

Parents log in securely with personal credentials to view their child's reports, attendance, and academic performance.

06

Submission Control

Admins open and close the submission window, enforce deadlines, and monitor which teachers have submitted scores.

07

Print-Ready Outputs

All reports formatted for A4 printing with school headers, footers, stamps, and signatures — ready to hand out.

08

Role-Based Access

Separate, secure dashboards for admins, teachers, students, and parents — each scoped to their specific permissions.

09

Deadline Alerts

Visual and system-level alerts when submission windows are approaching — no teacher misses their deadline.

How OPSMS Works

From login to printed report cards in four clean steps — designed to be fast, intuitive, and error-free.

1

Sign In

2

Enter Data

3

Auto-Calculate

OPSMS computes grades, rankings, and class-wide performance analytics instantly.

4

Generate Reports

Download or print professional A4 report cards and class summaries ready for distribution.

About OPSMS

Built Specifically for

The Online Result & Student Management System (OPSMS) was built to replace slow, paper-based academic administration with a fast, reliable digital alternative — tailored precisely for .

Running on Google Apps Script and Google Sheets, it costs nothing to host, works on any device, and requires zero technical maintenance — making it the most practical solution for a Tanzanian school setting.

Google Sheets Powered Mobile Friendly Role-Based Access A4 Print Ready Real-Time Data Zero Server Cost
Powered by Google Apps Script & Google Sheets — the most reliable, free, and widely accessible backend available for schools in Tanzania.
